Tutafy logo ideas

Five different directions for the owner to choose from. None is chosen yet. This page only shows them: nothing on the site has changed. After a pick, a separate ticket changes the logo everywhere (the list is at the bottom).

Not checked against trademarks. Nobody has searched trademark registers (US, EU or elsewhere) or domain and app-store name conflicts for any of these marks. None is copied from another company's logo as far as a designer can judge by eye, but that is not a legal check. Search before the chosen one is used in public.

At a glance

Each tile shows the icon on a light and a dark background. The first tile is today's logo, for comparison. Press an idea to jump to it.

Today's logo, and a thing worth knowing

Tutafy

The public site: the lucide "graduation cap" icon on an indigo tile, plus the word. It is a stock icon, not drawn for Tutafy.

Tutafy

The tutor app sidebar and the sign-up onboarding: a lightning bolt on a tile. A different mark again.

Finding: Tutafy has three different marks in use today: the graduation cap (public pages, share picture, certificate), a lightning bolt (tutor app sidebar, onboarding) and plain text with no mark (sign-in pages, invoice). Whatever you choose replaces all three, so it is worth fixing it once.

After you choose

A separate ticket will: put the chosen logo into SiteLogo and the app sidebar, the sign-in and onboarding pages, the certificate and the invoice; replace the favicon, the app icons and the share picture; and give you the list of outside accounts to update. Nothing changes before you pick.

Letters in all files are outlines made from open fonts (Manrope, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Sora and Outfit, all SIL Open Font License 1.1), so no font has to load. Colors: indigo #4F46E5 on light and #818CF8 on dark (the app's own brand colors) and one amber accent #F59E0B / #FBBF24.
