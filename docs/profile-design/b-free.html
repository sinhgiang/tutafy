About Daniel

Hallo! I'm Daniel, a German teacher in Berlin. For four years I've taught expats and exam candidates, and I make you speak from the first lesson. Grammar comes in when you need it, not before.

Before our first lesson I send a short level quiz. After each lesson you get a one-page summary: new words, the mistakes we fixed, and a small task for the next time.

Before you book

How does booking work

Pick a time, enter your name and email, and you are done. There is no account to create. Your confirmation email has the lesson link.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, free up to 24 hours before the lesson, with the link in your confirmation email. After that, Daniel may charge for the lesson.

How do I pay?

By card. Daniel sends a secure payment link with your confirmation.

What do I need for the lesson?

A laptop or phone with a camera and a quiet place. The lesson opens in your browser; there is nothing to install.
