How does booking work

Pick a time, enter your name and email, and you are done. There is no account to create. Your confirmation email has the lesson link.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, free up to 24 hours before the lesson, with the link in your confirmation email. After that, Priya may charge for the lesson.

How do I pay?

By card. Priya sends a secure payment link with your confirmation.

What do I need for the lesson?

A laptop or phone with a camera and a quiet place. The lesson opens in your browser; there is nothing to install.
