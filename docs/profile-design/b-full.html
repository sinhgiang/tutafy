About Maya

Hi, I'm Maya. I've coached IELTS and workplace English for eight years, first at a language school in Toronto and now one-to-one. Most of my students are busy professionals who need a score, a promotion, or both.

Lessons are practical. We start with a quick check of your level, then work from your real material: your essays, your presentations, your last meeting. You leave every lesson with three things to practise before we meet again.

How does booking work

Pick a time, enter your name and email, and you are done. There is no account to create. Your confirmation email has the lesson link.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, free up to 24 hours before the lesson, with the link in your confirmation email. After that, Maya may charge for the lesson.

How do I pay?

By card. Maya sends a secure payment link with your confirmation.

What do I need for the lesson?

A laptop or phone with a camera and a quiet place. The lesson opens in your browser; there is nothing to install.
