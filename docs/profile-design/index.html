Three designs for the public tutor profile

Today /tutors/<slug> has initials, a name, a time zone, one line of text and a generic “What to expect” box. Each design below adds a real photo, a one-sentence headline, price and trial lesson, reviews with lesson counts, an intro video, the next free times, and plain cancellation rules. Each has three states: full profile, new tutor, and Free plan.

A. Marketplace

Two columns with a booking card that stays in view. The most familiar layout for people who have used italki or Preply. Best for tutors with many reviews.

FullNew tutorFree plan

B. Personal page

One narrow column, a round photo on a cover, one big button. Made to be shared as a link on Instagram, WhatsApp or an email signature. Best for tutors who bring their own students.

FullNew tutorFree plan

C. Video-first

A dark hero with the intro video, a stats strip, section tabs and a week grid of times. The most polished first impression. Without a video it falls back to a large photo.
