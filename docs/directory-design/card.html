The tutor card

One card, four sizes of information. The photo and the next free time carry the decision; the price and the Book button sit together at the bottom.

  1. Photo4:3 crop of the tutor's photo. No photo: a soft coloured tile with initials, never a grey silhouette. The green “Trial $10” tag shows only when the tutor offers a trial lesson.
  2. Name and ratingStars and review count. A tutor with no reviews shows a quiet “New” pill, so an empty rating never looks like a bad one.
  3. One-sentence headlineTwo lines at most. Until the headline field exists (T-0761), the first sentence of the introduction is used.
  4. LanguagesWhat the tutor teaches, in the tutor's order.
  5. Next availableThe first free time in the visitor's own time zone (“Today 6:00 PM”, “Tomorrow 8:00 AM”, “Fri 10:00 AM”). It is the reason this tutor is listed; a card without one is never shown.
  6. Price and Book“From” the lowest lesson price, in the tutor's own currency. The whole photo is a link to the profile; the Book button goes straight to the booking page.
