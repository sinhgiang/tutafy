What you actually practise

Work English is a handful of situations that repeat. A tutor builds each one with you, you say it aloud, and you leave with sentences you can use that week.

Stand-up and status updates"We are blocked on the API change. I expect an answer by Thursday."
Asking someone to repeat"Sorry, could you go over the last point once more?"
Declining politely"I would like to help, but I cannot take this on before the 15th."
Giving feedback"Two things worked well. One thing I would change is the order."

A sample eight-week plan

One lesson a week. Your tutor will change the order to fit your job, but this shows what a good plan looks like.

  1. Week 1
    Your real work

    Bring two emails and one meeting. The tutor listens for the sentences you avoid and the ones you repeat.

  2. Week 2
    Meetings

    Opening, interrupting politely, asking for a repeat, and closing with next steps.

  3. Week 3
    Writing

    Clear emails: subject lines, one request per message, and a tone that is neither cold nor too casual.

  4. Week 4
    Presenting

    Structure a five-minute update, practise it aloud, and handle one hard question.

  5. Week 5
    Phone and video calls

    No facial cues: check understanding, summarise, and recover when the line drops.

  6. Week 6
    Small talk

    The first two minutes of a call and the coffee-machine conversation.

  7. Week 7
    Difficult conversations

    Saying no, giving feedback, and disagreeing without sounding rude.

  8. Week 8
    Run it yourself

    A mock meeting from start to finish, then a list of what to keep practising alone.

Questions about English for work

Do I need a tutor who works in my industry?
It helps, but it is not required. A good business English tutor asks for your real emails, slides and call notes and teaches from those. If your field has its own words (legal, medical, software), say so in the first message and look for a tutor who mentions it.
How long until I feel easier in meetings?
Most people notice small changes after four to six weeks of weekly lessons with practice between them, such as writing your next three emails in English before the lesson. It depends on your starting level and how much you speak at work.
Can I book lessons before or after work?
Yes. Tutors on this page publish open times in the next 14 days, shown in your own time zone. Early morning and evening times are common; use the Free times filter to see only those.
Will my company pay for it?
Some employers cover language lessons as training. Tutors can send an invoice for each lesson; ask your manager or HR what they need to see.
