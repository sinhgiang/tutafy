One family, Geist, which the app already loads. No uppercase tracking labels except in the facts row.

Space, shape, targets

  • 8 px grid: 8 · 12 · 16 · 24. Card padding 24 (18 on a phone). Gap between cards 24 (16).
  • Radius: 16 cards, 12 buttons and inputs, 10 time chips, full for day circles.
  • Every tap target is 40 px or more (day circles 40, times 44, buttons 44 to 52). The old page had 36 px days and 11 px help text.
  • Focus ring: 3 px indigo glow on every control. Colour is never the only signal (selected day is filled, not only tinted).

Rules for empty, loading and error

  • Empty: say what is missing, then what to do. “Choose a day and time” on the button, never a dead grey panel.
  • Loading: grey blocks in the real shape (see States). Respect reduced motion.
  • Error: what happened, whether anything was saved, one button. Next to the field it belongs to.
  • Not filled in by the tutor: the section is hidden, or says so honestly (no reviews yet), never fake content.
