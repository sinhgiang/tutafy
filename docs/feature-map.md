# Feature map

Tutafy: booking, lessons and payments for private tutors (Next.js 16, Supabase, Polar for plans).

## Features

- Earnings (T-0821, G2 of `docs/dashboard-redesign/`; stacked on the shell branch of T-0820): `/earnings` (`src/app/(dashboard)/earnings/page.tsx`) replaces Payments and Reports, which now redirect to its tabs (`/payments` to `?tab=payments`, `/reports` to `?tab=reports`); the menu has one "Earnings" item (17 pages). It fills the shell's frame with a 12-column grid (`.dash-grid` in `src/styles/dashboard-shell.css`: KPI cards span 3, the chart 8, the ranking 4, the table 12; two across under 1280px). Tabs: Overview (Earned, Still owed, Lessons, Cancellation rate, each with a trend against the period before, a bar chart per week or month, top students, recent payments), Payments (the history with a status filter, 100 rows shown, invoice links, the Stripe card) and Reports (Pro: attendance and the accounting exports; the gate is `requireFeature('pro', 'Reports')` before any lesson is read). A period switch (30 days, 90 days, 12 months, all time; anything else in the address is 90 days) drives everything; all time has no trend ("No comparison"), and a trend is never invented when the earlier period had nothing. Export CSV (`src/app/api/earnings/export/route.ts`) writes the history for the period and status on screen; the tutor is the session's, a spreadsheet formula in a student name is defused (`csvCell`). Empty ("No earnings yet", a button to schedule a lesson) and error ("We could not load your earnings", Try again; never a page of zeros) states. The numbers are in `src/lib/earnings.ts` (pure). `tests/earnings.test.ts` runs the real page and the real export over a stand-in Supabase, including a second tutor and the Free-plan lock. The Dashboard home was not redrawn: it already sits in the frame and is full width; its own redesign (today's agenda) is not part of this ticket.
- Students and Lessons lists (T-0822, G3 of `docs/dashboard-redesign/`): `/students` (`src/app/(dashboard)/students/page.tsx`, rows in `StudentsTable.tsx`) and `/lessons` (`lessons/page.tsx`, `LessonsClient.tsx`) are server-paged lists, 25 rows a page (the database is asked for one page and a count; `src/lib/list-params.ts`, `student-list.ts`, `lesson-list.ts`). Search (name or email; for lessons the student's name), filters (students: level, status; lessons: when, lesson status, payment status, and `?student=<id>` from the student page's "See all lessons"), sort, a pager with real links, "Clear filters", Export CSV of ALL matching rows for the filters on screen (`/api/students/export`, `/api/lessons/export`, up to 5000), and a bulk bar above the table (students: set active, paused or inactive through `POST /api/students/bulk`; lessons: mark paid or completed through `POST /api/lessons/bulk`; both check the ids are uuids, at most 100, update with the session's `tutor_id` in the same statement and answer the number of rows really changed). Everything in the address goes through a fixed list or a strict pattern (`pick`, `parsePage`, `isUuid`), and search words lose every character that means something in a filter string (`cleanSearch`), so an address cannot add a filter. The toolbar is a plain GET form (works without scripts). A row opens with Enter or a click, the name is a real link so Tab reaches it, every checkbox has a name. Under 768px a row becomes a card (`.dash-table.is-cards`), no sideways scroll. Empty, no-match, past-the-last-page and error states each have their own words (a failed read is an error, never an empty list). CSV cells that start with = + - or @ are defused (`src/lib/csv.ts`). `tests/lists.test.ts` runs the real pages and routes over 120 made-up students and a second tutor (`tests/support/fake-db.ts` gained `or()` and `count` for it). Not in this ticket: the side panel, saved tabs, and the "next lesson" and "owes" columns of the sample (they need joins the tables do not have a cheap form for).
- Calendar and Messages (T-0823, G4 of `docs/dashboard-redesign/`; stacked on the lists branch of T-0822): `/calendar` (`src/app/(dashboard)/calendar/page.tsx`, `CalendarView.tsx`, rules in `src/lib/calendar.ts`) opens on the WEEK (`?view=week|month&date=YYYY-MM-DD`; a bad value is week and today): a time grid 6:00 to 22:00, seven day columns from Sunday, each lesson a block at its time; the server reads only the signed-in tutor's lessons for the days drawn. A scheduled lesson can be DRAGGED to a new day and time (snaps to 15 minutes, stays inside the grid) or moved with a "Move to" date-time form (keyboard and phone); both call `POST /api/lessons/reschedule` (`src/app/api/lessons/reschedule/route.ts`), which moves the lesson only if it is the tutor's own and still scheduled (the tutor id is the session's, in the same update; the end time follows the start), and answers 409 `overlap` with the lessons it would overlap when the new time clashes with another of the tutor's lessons; the view then shows "That time overlaps ..." with "Move anyway" (sends `force: true`) and "Keep the old time". Cancelled lessons take no time and never conflict; a lesson that starts when another ends is not an overlap. Under 768px the week grid is replaced by a day-by-day list. Not done: Google Calendar and the student are not told about a move (the sync route is separate), availability is not shaded on the grid, and times are read in the browser's time zone, as the old month grid did. `/messages` is a workspace (`messages/layout.tsx` loads the list with `src/lib/conversations.ts`; `MessagesShell.tsx` draws it): from 1280px the conversation list (320px), the open thread and a student card (280px, `student-card`) sit side by side and the old 760px cap is gone; below 1280px the list shows first and a conversation replaces it (`data-open`), with a Back link in the thread. The list has a filter box, unread counts, and states for no active students and a failed read. `tests/calendar-messages.test.ts` runs the real pages and the move route over two tutors (the second tutor cannot move, see or open the first tutor's lessons, students or messages).

- Tutor profile photo (T-0766, the photo bucket is written but NOT applied: see below): a tutor adds, changes or removes their photo in Settings, Profile (`src/components/settings/AvatarUploader.tsx`). The browser draws the chosen picture again on a canvas and saves it as a JPEG no wider than 1200 pixels (`src/lib/avatar-client.ts`: this applies the way a phone turned it and drops every hidden note), then sends it as base64 in JSON to `POST /api/tutors/avatar` (`src/app/api/tutors/avatar/route.ts`; `DELETE` removes the photo). The route trusts nothing about the file: it reads the real type from the first bytes (`sniffImage` in `src/lib/avatar-image.ts`: JPEG, PNG and WebP only, never SVG or GIF, never the name or a label), counts the size on the decoded bytes (`MAX_AVATAR_BYTES`, 2 MB; a body that says it is over 3 MB is refused before it is read), and cuts the hidden notes (EXIF, GPS, camera, author) out of the bytes (`stripMetadata`). Who may write: the signed-in tutor only, for their own photo only. The tutor id is the session's and is never read from the body; the file goes in the tutor's own folder `<tutor id>/<random name>.<ext>` of the public bucket `tutor-avatars`; `tutors.avatar_url` is updated with the tutor's OWN session (row level security: their own row) and, if that fails, the new file is taken back; the old file is removed only when its address is in the tutor's own folder (`ownAvatarPath`). Same-origin calls only, 10 uploads in 10 minutes for one tutor (in this instance's memory, checked in `tests/rate-limits.test.ts`). The bucket is made by `supabase/migrations/024_tutor_avatars.sql` (public, 2 MB, three picture types, NO policy for anon or authenticated, so no browser can write to it; only the server's own key uploads). THE FILE IS WRITTEN BUT NOT APPLIED: the owner runs it in the Supabase SQL Editor; until then the route answers 503 "Photo upload is not set up yet" and nothing is created at run time (the old lesson-materials route makes its bucket on the fly; this one never does). The photo shows through `tutors.avatar_url` on the public profile and the booking page (only an https address is drawn: `httpsImage`), the dashboard header, and, once the directory of T-0769 is merged, on the directory card. `PHOTO_UPLOAD_READY` in `src/lib/profile-completion.ts` is now true: the photo step of the profile-completion card is worth 20 and sends the tutor to Settings, Profile; the other steps are worth hours 25, introduction 20, price 20, review 15. Settings keeps the photo out of the form it saves, so pressing Save cannot write an old address over a new one. A tutor can still write any https address into their own `avatar_url` by calling the database directly (the existing policy lets them update their own row): the photo is never trusted as content, only drawn as a picture. Tests (all in `npm test`): `tests/avatar-image.test.ts` (type, size, hidden notes, own-folder rule), `tests/tutor-avatar-route.test.ts` (the real route: who may write, a second tutor, refused files, replace and remove, failures, no bucket made at run time), `tests/tutor-avatars-migration.test.ts` (PGlite with a stand-in of Supabase's storage tables: the bucket, its limits, no write policy), `tests/avatar-ui.test.ts` (the box in each state), and the updated `tests/profile-completion.test.ts`.
