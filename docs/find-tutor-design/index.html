Find me a tutor, and the Verified badge

Two ideas for Tutafy's tutor directory, three designs each. Every design is shown on a computer and on a 390 px phone, and the Light, Dark and System buttons above switch the colours. Pick one design for each part (or tell me what to mix) and I will build it.

1 · "Find me a tutor": we suggest 3 tutors you can book today

A student who does not want to browse answers a few questions and gets three tutors, each with the reasons it was picked. It uses the same filters as the directory (language, level, price, free times, trial lesson), so it works on the real list of tutors who can be booked. No account is needed.

My suggestion: A. International learners who are comfortable with software like to see the answer change beside the question, it is one screen to build on top of the directory filters, and on a phone it is a form followed by the three tutors. B converts well on phones but hides the result until the end. C is the lightest, but a sentence with blanks is harder to make accessible.
A

One screen: the form, and the 3 tutors beside it

The form on the left, the three tutors on the right. On a phone the tutors sit under the form and the button stays in reach.

Good for

  • Fast: all answers visible at once, easy to change one
  • Same fields as the directory filters, so least to build
  • Works without scripts: it can be a plain form that opens a results page

Watch out for

  • A long form on a phone
  • Five fields may feel like work for a student who only wants to browse
B

One question at a time, then the 3 tutors

Four short screens with big answers to tap, a progress bar and Back. The last screen is the result, with "Edit answers".

Good for

  • Feels easy on a phone: one thing to decide at a time
  • Each screen can explain its question
  • Easy to track where people stop

Watch out for

  • More taps, and the result is hidden until the end
  • Four screens to build and test
C

A sentence you complete

"I want to learn Spanish as a beginner, paying up to $30 a lesson, with free times in the evening." Tap a blank to change it. The tutors appear under it.

Good for

  • The lightest: it can sit at the top of the directory
  • Reads like a person talking, which suits the brand

Watch out for

  • Blanks inside a sentence need careful work for screen readers and keyboards
  • Hard to add a sixth question later

2 · Verified badge and intro video on a tutor profile

Students pay a stranger. A badge that means something, and a short video where they can hear the tutor, are the two things that lower that worry most. The three designs differ in how much they explain. The badge also shows on the directory card.

My suggestion: B. This audience researches before they trust, so a panel that lists exactly what was checked (and when) is more convincing than a badge alone, and the video sits in the About section where a person is already reading. A is the lightest to build. C puts the video first, which is the most striking, but a badge on a photo says less.
A

Video first, a "Verified" badge by the name that explains itself

The intro video is the first thing on the profile. The Verified badge next to the name opens a small card that says what was checked.

Good for

  • The video gets the most attention
  • The badge is one small component, easy to reuse on cards
  • The profile layout barely changes

Watch out for

  • The explanation is hidden until tapped
  • A video that autoplays is not allowed, so the poster must be good

What I need from you

1. Pick one design for each part

Part 1: A, B or C. Part 2: A, B or C. You can also say "A for the form, B for the result cards".

2. What "Verified" should mean (a decision, not a design)

  • Light: email confirmed, booking page working, introduction written, 10 lessons held on Tutafy. Automatic and free. It says "Established", not "identity checked".
  • Standard (drawn in the designs): identity checked from a government ID, plus email confirmed, plus 25 lessons held. The ID check is done by hand by us (free, slow) or by a provider (a fee for each check: a money decision for you).
  • I will not write the word "Verified" in the product until the check really exists, so a badge is never ahead of the truth.

3. The intro video

  • Link to YouTube or Vimeo (my suggestion): the tutor pastes a link, we embed it. No storage cost, and tutors already have videos.
  • Upload to Tutafy: a smoother experience but it costs storage and bandwidth, and needs size limits and checks.

Notes on all six designs

  • Words are English only. Targets are at least 44 px. Text is at least 4.5 to 1 against its background in both colour schemes. Nothing relies on colour alone: the badge always carries a shield and the word "Verified" or a text alternative.
  • Photos are initials tiles, like the live directory. Video is a poster with a play button: no video autoplays and every video has captions.
