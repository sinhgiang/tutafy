# T-1006: the two small pieces left of stage B - niche pages on /tutors, "What it is for" on /find-a-tutor

Stage B of T-1006 (directory filters and niche pages) was built by T-1033 (pull request #110, `docs/features/T-1033-niche-pages.md`): the filters, `/learn/<language>/<group>` and the sitemap lines. Its "Not done" list named two small gaps. This closes them. There is no migration and nothing is read that was not read before.

- **`/tutors` lists the niche pages.** Under the "Browse tutors by language" list, a second list "Browse tutors by reason for learning" links the niche pages that exist right now, most tutors first, each with its tutor count ("English for work and career 4"). It asks the same function as the sitemap and the language pages (`pickPairPages`, `src/lib/niche-pages.ts`: a written guide, at least 3 tutors who can be booked, at most 3 pages), so no empty page is linked and the list is not drawn at all while no pair qualifies. Files: `src/app/tutors/(directory)/page.tsx`.
- **`/find-a-tutor` uses `for=`.** A fifth question, "What it is for" (Any and the six groups of `src/lib/tutor-groups.ts`), is the directory's own "Learning for" filter. `formFilters` keeps `group` (it is read from `?for=` by `parseFilters` from the fixed list, so any other value is "Any"); the question is drawn only once a tutor who can be booked has ticked a group, or when the address already carries `for=` (the same rule the directory uses to show its filter); the picks are the best three of the tutors who ticked the group; a reason "Teaches students learning for work and career" is printed only for a tutor who ticked it; "removing <group> (N tutors)" is offered when fewer than three fit; "See all tutors" keeps `for=`; "Start again" clears it. The page's lead says "A few quick answers" (it said "Five") and the first FAQ answer names the new answer. Files: `src/lib/find-tutor.ts`, `src/components/find/FindTutorView.tsx`, `src/lib/site-content.ts`.
- **Tests.** `tests/niche-listing.test.ts` (11 tests) runs the real pages over made-up tutors with a private email, phone and payment link in their rows: the list and its order and counts, each linked address opens a real page, no list while no pair qualifies or nobody ticked, the five questions and six radio buttons, no question while nobody ticked, `?for=work` picks only tutors who ticked work, every reason true, the address read from the fixed list (a wrong case, a list, markup), fewer picks with the way to drop it, an explained state for a group nobody teaches, `formFilters`, and nothing private on either page. `tests/find-tutor.test.ts` and `tests/niche-pages.test.ts` are unchanged and pass. Mutations tried (the group dropped from `formFilters`, the list not drawn, the question always asked, a reason without the tick, a page without three tutors listed): each turns tests red.
- **Not done.** Nothing shows on the live site until the directory opens and tutors tick groups (0 have, 30 Oct 2026). Kids and teens, University and Conversation still have no niche page. The finder does not ask for a price range or a day.
