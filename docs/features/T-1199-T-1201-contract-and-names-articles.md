# T-1199 and T-1201: tutoring contract template and tutoring business names (blog articles, dated 6 Nov 2026)

Two traffic articles for the 6 Nov 2026 calendar day, each 3,500 to 4,000 words with three credited photos. Both are bodies in `src/lib/blog-posts/`, registered at the end of `POSTS` in `src/lib/blog.ts` with date `2026-11-06`, with a hero alt text, a `page-meta.ts` entry and a hero drawn by `scripts/gen-blog-images.mjs`.

- `/blog/tutoring-contract-template` (T-1199, keyword "tutoring contract template", 170 searches a month in the US). Step 0: the older guide `tutoring-cancellation-policy-and-contracts` says what a policy should contain but has no contract text, so this is a new article with a different angle: a copyable twelve-clause one-page agreement with blanks, a reason for each clause, versions for adults, children, groups and packages, signing (US E-SIGN Act), and outside rules that can override a clause (FTC Cooling-Off Rule, UK 14-day cancellation rights, a fee that looks like a penalty). It says what Tutafy stores and does not (a contract box with tick and typed name; the time of agreement is saved; no copy of the text, no parent signature, no PDF, no automatic late fee). The older guide got one "Related guide" link to the new one.
- `/blog/tutoring-business-names` (T-1201, keyword "tutoring business names", 170 searches a month). Step 0: no post is about naming, so it is new: five naming styles, eight tests, a five-step lookup check (plain search, state registry, USPTO trademark search, domain, social handles), the SBA's four ways to register or protect a name, the UK company-name and trade-mark rules, and what Tutafy has (one name field, a booking link built from the sign-up name plus four random characters, a custom domain on Pro) and does not (no business-name field, no checker, no domain sales). Every example name is invented and unchecked, and the article says so. Nothing is bought or registered.
