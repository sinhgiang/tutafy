- The standard trial lesson (T-1007, the owner's choice W-3173 option 1; the rule is in `src/lib/trial.ts`): a trial lesson is always 30 minutes, the tutor still switches it on and sets its price in Settings (`tutors.trial_enabled`, `tutors.trial_price`: no new column and no migration), and it is for a first lesson only. Everywhere a visitor or a tutor reads about it the words come from the same file: the directory card says "30-min trial $10" on the photo (`src/components/directory/TutorCard.tsx`, `data-testid="card-trial"`; on a phone the badge wraps to two lines inside the 112px photo), the profile and the booking page say "30-minute trial $10" (`src/app/tutors/[slug]/page.tsx`, `src/components/booking/BookingSections.tsx`, whose facts row reads "$10 for 30 min"), the page title, description and FAQ say it too (`src/lib/booking-page.ts`, `src/lib/profile-answers.ts`, `src/lib/tutor-profile.ts` profile notes), the directory and Find-me-a-tutor filters say "A 30-minute first lesson" and the "Find me a tutor" reasons say "30-minute trial for $8" (`src/lib/find-tutor.ts`). Settings, section "Trial lesson and contract" (`src/components/settings/sections/ExtrasSection.tsx`), tells the tutor the rule next to the price (`data-testid="trial-rule"`) and the live booking-page preview names the trial the same way. On the booking card (`src/app/book/[slug]/BookingForm.tsx`) switching the trial on locks the lesson length to one button, "30 min", clears the chosen time (the free times are worked out for 30 minutes) and shows the trial price at the top; every length the form uses is read from one line, `const duration = isTrial ? TRIAL_MINUTES : chosenDuration`. The rule is kept by the server, not by the page (`src/app/api/bookings/route.ts`): a request with `isTrial: true` is saved as 30 minutes at the trial price whatever length the browser sends (and needs no length), is refused with 400 when the tutor has not switched a trial on at a price above zero (before, it was saved as a regular-price lesson that only said "trial"), and is refused with 400 when the student already has a lesson with this tutor that was not cancelled. `isTrial` must be the boolean `true`; the string "true" is a regular lesson. `tests/standard-trial.test.ts` runs the real route over a fake database (a second tutor, a student of another tutor, a cancelled earlier lesson, a coupon on a trial), the real card and the real Settings section, and was run against the old route to see the new tests fail (7 of 12). What is not done: a student who gives a different email address for a second trial is not recognised (the check is on the tutor's own student record, found by email), and a tutor who already has trial lessons saved at other lengths keeps them as they are (no data is changed).
