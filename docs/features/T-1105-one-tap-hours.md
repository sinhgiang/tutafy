# T-1105: One-tap free hours for tutors who have none

A tutor with no weekly free hours can press one of four ready-made weeks instead of drawing a week. The press writes the hours for the signed-in tutor and the "Choose your free hours" step turns to done.

- **Why.** Of the 24 tutors with a booking page, an introduction and a link, 21 had no hours (count of 2026-10-30). They are all older accounts: the hours step of onboarding failed silently before T-0728 (2026-10-21) and showed "You're all set" anyway; none of the 21 has signed in since 2026-10-23 and 20 only came on the day they registered, so the dashboard cards of T-0739 and T-1043 never reached them. For a tutor who does come back, drawing a week on the Availability grid, or filling the day, from and to boxes one slot at a time, was the next wall.
- **What a tutor sees.** The "Get ready for the tutor directory" card on the dashboard (T-1043) now carries, inside its first step, a box "Pick a week in one tap" with four buttons: Weekday evenings (Mon to Fri 18:00 to 21:00), Weekday mornings (Mon to Fri 08:00 to 12:00), Weekdays, 9 to 5 (Mon to Fri 09:00 to 17:00) and Weekends (Sat and Sun 09:00 to 17:00). The box names the tutor's time zone and links to where it is changed (Settings, Booking page) and to the Availability page for custom hours. The empty Availability page shows the same box. After a press the box says what was saved and the page loads again. A tutor who has any hours sees neither.
- **Files.** `src/lib/hours-presets.ts` (the four presets, `parseHoursPreset`, `applyHoursPreset`), `src/app/api/availability/preset/route.ts` (`POST {preset}`), `src/components/availability/QuickHours.tsx` (the box), `src/components/dashboard/DirectorySetupCard.tsx` and `src/app/(dashboard)/availability/page.tsx` (where it sits), `src/lib/directory-setup.ts` (now also reads `tutors.timezone` to name the zone).
- **Rules.** The preset id is looked up in the fixed list (anything else is 400). The tutor is the session's, never the body's. Same-origin only (403 otherwise), 401 when signed out. Hours are written ONLY for a tutor who has none (409 otherwise), so a preset can never double up or replace hours a tutor chose. The rows carry `tutor_id`, `day_of_week` (0 = Sunday), `start_time`, `end_time` and nothing else, and are written with the tutor's own session so row level security applies. Every answer is read; a failed write is taken back; if two presses race (two tabs) the rows of the later check are removed and the answer is 409. Nothing is sent to anyone and there is no migration.
- **Tests.** `tests/hours-presets.test.ts` (19 tests: the list, strict id parsing, the rule on an in-memory database, a second tutor's hours, failed reads and writes, the race, and the real route with 401, 403, 400, 413, a body naming another tutor, a second press, and the card with and without hours). Mutations tried: the tutor filter removed from the check, the "already has hours" refusal removed, the race check removed; each turns tests red.
- **Not done.** The new-tutor onboarding step is unchanged (it already offers Mon to Fri 09:00 to 17:00 and, since T-0728, tells the tutor when it cannot save). A preset is not offered to a tutor who already has some hours (they use the Availability grid). The reminder letter for the 21 tutors is drafted in the Brain inbox and was not sent.
