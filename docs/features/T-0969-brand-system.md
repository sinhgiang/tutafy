# T-0969: logo D2 everywhere, and one brand source

The owner chose logo D2 (a mortarboard in an indigo app tile, an amber tassel) on 25 Oct 2026. It replaces the three different marks the site had (the lucide cap in the top bar, the lightning bolt in the app sidebar and onboarding, a hand-pasted cap in the certificate, a bare word on sign-in and invoices).

**One source.** `src/lib/brand.ts` holds the colours (light and dark), the font (Geist), the type scale, corners, spacing and the logo shapes. `npm run brand:build` (`scripts/build-brand.mjs`, builders in `tests/support/brand-files.ts`) writes from it: `src/styles/brand-tokens.css` (the `--brand-*` variables, loaded by `src/app/globals.css`, with Tailwind names `bg-brand`, `text-brand`, `bg-tassel`), the colour blocks of the tutor app shell (between the `brand:begin` and `brand:end` marks of `src/styles/dashboard-shell.css`), the tables of `docs/brand/BRAND.md`, the logo set in `public/brand/` (SVG and PNG; light, dark, one colour, horizontal, icon, maskable, push badge), the browser and app icons (`src/app/icon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-icon.png`, `public/icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`) and the one-page sheet `public/brand/brand-sheet.html` (picture: `public/brand/tutafy-brand-sheet.png`). The word "Tutafy" in the horizontal logo is Geist Bold as outlines (`src/lib/brand-wordmark.ts`).

**Components.** `BrandMark` (icon) and `BrandLogo` (horizontal) in `src/components/brand/`, theme `light`, `dark` or `auto` (follows the app's dark theme through `--brand-logo-*`). `SiteLogo` (every public page and the footer, always a link to `/`) now draws `BrandLogo`. Sidebar: icon on the narrow rail, horizontal logo from 1280 px. Also: sign-in and sign-up, student header, onboarding, offline page, "Made with Tutafy" line, certificate, invoice (inline SVG), the picture of the sidebar on the home page.

**Files and metadata.** The manifest has `any` and `maskable` icons and the brand colours; the browser colour is the brand primary; the share picture (`/og`) is the horizontal dark logo on the dark brand surface; the push badge is the white shape; the service worker cache is `tutafy-v2` so no page or picture with the old logo is served from it; the proxy leaves `/brand/`, `/icon.svg` and `/apple-icon` alone.

**Share pictures of the sub-pages.** Every page reads `/og`; a blog post reads its own hero (`public/blog/<slug>-hero.webp` and `.avif`). The 25 heroes carried a third old mark (a white tile with a triangle, and a bolt in the "Tutafy vs alternatives" one); `scripts/gen-blog-images.mjs` (run with `node --import ./tests/register.mjs scripts/gen-blog-images.mjs`) now draws the brand logo in white and the heroes were regenerated. `/switch` wrote its own `openGraph` and so had no share picture at all; it now names `/og` (`SOCIAL_IMAGE` is exported from `src/lib/page-meta.ts`). Real tags were read from the running app for `/`, about, contact, blog list, a post, tutors, find a tutor, switch, updates, privacy and sign-in.

**Checks.** `tests/brand.test.ts` (it also compares the corner of every hero, both formats, with the logo, and fails a page that writes `openGraph` without the share picture): every generated file on disk equals what `brand.ts` builds (a colour changed in one place, or a generated file edited by hand, fails); the React logo has the shapes of the files; no logo under the smallest size; manifest, browser colour, share picture, service worker and structured data are the brand's; the old marks (a bolt or a cap in an indigo tile, the lucide cap path, the hand-pasted cap) are not back, and `Zap` and `GraduationCap` are allowed only in listed files as ordinary icons; brand colours typed as hex outside the source can only get fewer (a counted list: the e-mail templates and a few charts still carry some). Five deliberate breakages were run against it and each failed.

**Not done, and why.** The e-mail templates (16) keep their text banner: they never had a logo picture, and a logo in mail changes what real people receive. The public pages still use Tailwind's own indigo scale for buttons. The earlier update pictures and blog heroes show the old logo and stay as history. The owner's accounts (Polar, Stripe, Google consent screen, Vercel and GitHub avatars, social profiles, Supabase mail templates) need the new icon uploaded by hand. A trademark search has not been done. The full list is section 6 of `docs/brand/BRAND.md`.
