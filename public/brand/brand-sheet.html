Space, size and misuse

Clear space and smallest size

Keep a quarter of the icon's height free on every side (the corner radius of the tile). Nothing enters that space. The icon is never drawn under 16 px; the horizontal logo is never lower than 24 px high.

Do not

✕ Stretch or squash it

✕ Change its colours

✕ Rotate or tilt it

✕ Add a shadow or outline

✕ Put the full-colour icon on a surface of its own colour

Tutafy

✕ Set the word in another font
