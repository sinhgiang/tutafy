- The calendar opens on the current week, with a time grid from 6 in the morning to 10 at night. Switch to Month whenever you like.
- Drag a scheduled lesson to another day or time. It snaps to 15 minutes. On a phone or with a keyboard, use the Move to form instead.
- If the new time overlaps another lesson, Tutafy tells you and lets you choose to move it anyway or keep the old time.
- On a phone the week becomes a simple day-by-day list.
- Messages now shows your conversations, the open chat and a card about the student side by side on a wide screen.
- The conversation list has a filter box and shows how many messages you have not read.
