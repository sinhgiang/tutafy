- In Settings, on the Profile tab, press Upload photo and pick a clear photo of your face (JPG, PNG or WebP).
- Tutafy makes the photo smaller for you and removes hidden details such as where it was taken.
- Your photo shows on your public booking page, and the photo step on your dashboard ticks itself once it is there.
- You can change or remove the photo at any time.
