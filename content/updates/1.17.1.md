- Spanish, English and other popular languages now have a page of their own, listing the tutors who teach that language and have open times in the next 14 days.
- Each page starts with a short answer, then explains how learning that language with a tutor works and what to look for in a tutor.
- Links on the page narrow the list by level, trial lesson, price or evening times.
- A language appears only when enough tutors teach it, so you never land on an empty page.
- The tutor directory links to these pages.
