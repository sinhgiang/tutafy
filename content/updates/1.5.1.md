title: A reminder when your booking page has no hours
summary: If you have not set your free hours yet, your dashboard now tells you and takes you to the page where you pick them.
---
- If your booking page has no free hours, students cannot book you. Your dashboard now shows a clear note at the top with an "Add your hours" button.
- The note goes away as soon as you add your first hour. Nothing is changed for you, and we never pick hours on your behalf.
