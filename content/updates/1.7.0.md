- The directory only lists tutors a student can book today, and it opens once six tutors are ready.
- Until then the page explains this and invites tutors to finish their profile, instead of showing a nearly empty list.
- Your dashboard card now says "Ready" when you meet every condition, and your card appears the moment the directory opens.
- The conditions have not changed: booking page on, a name, an introduction of at least 80 characters and at least one free time.
