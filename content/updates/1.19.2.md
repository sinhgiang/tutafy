- Marketplaces like Preply and iTalki take 15–33% of every lesson. Preply also keeps your first trial lesson in full; iTalki takes 0% of trial lessons.
- iTalki is not a flat 15%. It takes 15–21% depending on the lesson type, and 0% on trial lessons. The articles that said otherwise are fixed.
- Preply is 33% for a new tutor, falling to 18% as you teach more hours.
- Every page that shows these numbers now says when they were checked (25 Oct 2026) and links to Preply's and iTalki's own pages, so you can look for yourself.
