- New Level filter: Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced. Tutors choose the levels they teach in Settings, Profile, and the levels show on their card.
- New Time zone panel: pick the zone you want times shown in, and switch on "Within 3 hours of me" to see only tutors whose day is close to yours.
- The page address remembers every filter, so a list you send to a friend opens exactly as you left it.
- When no tutor matches, the page says so and offers to remove the filter that helps most.
- The tutor directory now has a dark theme that follows your device setting.
