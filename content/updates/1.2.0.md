- In Settings, on the Booking page tab, tick any of 30, 45, 60 and 90 minutes to say which lessons you teach.
- Pick the length that is already selected when a student opens your booking page.
- Your booking page, your team members' pages and the booking widget on your own website show only the lengths you ticked.
- If you do not choose, students still see all four lengths, with 60 minutes first. Your prices do not change.
