- Students can only book you inside your free hours. Until you add some, your booking page has nothing to pick.
- The card at the top of your dashboard now has four buttons: weekday evenings, weekday mornings, weekdays 9 to 5, and weekends. One press saves the whole week.
- The box shows the time zone your hours are in, with a link to change it.
- The empty Availability page has the same four buttons. You can change or remove any hour afterwards.
- If you already have hours, you will not see the buttons and nothing of yours changes.
