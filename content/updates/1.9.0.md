- Payments and Reports are now one page called Earnings, with Overview, Payments and Reports tabs.
- The Overview shows what you earned, what is still owed, lessons taught and your cancellation rate, each compared with the period before.
- Switch between the last 30 days, 90 days, 12 months or all time, and everything on the page follows.
- A chart shows your earnings week by week, next to your top students and your most recent payments.
- Press Export CSV to download the payments you see on screen for your own records.
- The old Payments and Reports links still work and take you to the matching tab.
