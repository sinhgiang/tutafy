- Drag down a day column to draw your free hours. The block snaps to half hours and is saved when you let go.
- Press an empty spot to open a small form with an hour already filled in, then press Add slot.
- Press a block to change its day or times, or to remove it. Drag its top or bottom edge to start earlier or end later.
- You can do everything without a mouse: every day name is a button that adds a slot to that day, and every block opens its form with the keyboard.
- On a phone, tap an empty spot or a day name. If a new time overlaps hours you already have, Tutafy tells you and changes nothing.
