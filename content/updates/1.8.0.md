- All dashboard pages share one frame, centred on wide screens, so nothing is stuck to the left edge of a big monitor.
- The side menu stays in view while you scroll. On a narrower window it shrinks to icons, and on a phone it moves to the bottom.
- Press Ctrl K (Cmd K on a Mac) to open the command bar. Type a page or an action, such as Schedule a lesson, and press Enter.
- The page title in the top bar now matches the page you are on.
- Choose Light, Dark or your device setting for the look of the dashboard.
- Loading and error screens look the same on every page, with a button to try again.
