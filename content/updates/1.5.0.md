- You can now pinch to zoom on your phone on every page, so small text never stays small.
- Small grey text on the home page, the blog, the booking pages and tutor profiles is darker and easier to read.
- Every public page marks its main content, and the comparison tables and sliders in blog posts have proper labels, which helps screen readers and keyboard users.
- Links inside sentences are underlined, so they stand out without relying on colour.
