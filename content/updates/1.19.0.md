- A tutor can paste a link to a video on YouTube or Vimeo in Settings, under Profile. It shows at the top of their public profile.
- The video stays on YouTube or Vimeo. Nothing loads from those sites until a visitor presses play, and a video never starts by itself.
- Only YouTube and Vimeo links work. Any other link is refused with a clear message.
- Tutors who meet four conditions get an Established badge next to their name: the sign-in email is confirmed, the booking page is live, the introduction is written, and at least 10 lessons have been taught on Tutafy.
- Press the badge to see each check with its date. The card also says what the badge is not: Tutafy does not check IDs, diplomas or qualifications.
- The badge and a Video label also show on the tutor cards when you browse tutors.
- The profile has a short opening line about what the tutor teaches and what a lesson costs, a short list of questions with answers, and a dark theme that follows your device setting.
- The badge and the video start to show once the matching database update has been applied.
