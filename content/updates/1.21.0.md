summary: A trial lesson is now always 30 minutes. The tutor still sets the price, and it is for a student's first lesson only.

---
- A trial lesson is 30 minutes for every tutor. Students know what they get before they book, and can compare trials between tutors.
- Tutor cards, profiles and booking pages now say "30-minute trial" with the price. Switching the trial on in the booking form sets the length to 30 minutes and the free times follow it.
- A trial is for a student's first lesson with that tutor. A student who already has a lesson is asked to book a regular one. A cancelled lesson does not count.
- Tutors still switch the trial on and set its price in Settings. If a trial is off, it can no longer be booked.
