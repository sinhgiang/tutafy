- A new "Get your first student" card at the top of your dashboard walks you through the five steps that bring in a first booking.
- The steps are: add a student, set your free hours, choose your lesson lengths, paste your card payment link, and share your booking page.
- Steps tick themselves as you finish them, and each one has a button that takes you to the right page.
- Card payment links are part of the Pro plan, so on the Free plan that step is shown but not required.
- The card goes away when you are done, or sooner if you press Hide.
