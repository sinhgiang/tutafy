- Search students by name or email, and lessons by the student's name.
- Filter students by level and status. Filter lessons by when they are, their status and whether they are paid.
- Sort any column, move through pages of 25, and press Clear filters to start again.
- Tick several rows to change them at once: set students active, paused or inactive, or mark lessons paid or completed.
- Press Export CSV to download every row that matches your filters, not only the page you see.
- On a phone each row becomes a card, so nothing slides sideways.
