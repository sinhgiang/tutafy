- Profile, Booking page, Payments, Trial and contract, Integrations and Plan each start with a heading and one sentence about what is inside.
- A section loads the first time you open it, so Settings opens faster.
- Switching sections never loses what you have typed. The Save changes bar and the live preview of your booking page keep working across all of them.
- The address shows the section you are in, so reloading the page or sending the link brings you back to the same place.
