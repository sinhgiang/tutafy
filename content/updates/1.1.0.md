- New guide: how to create a Stripe Payment Link and paste it into Tutafy so students see a "Pay by card" button.
- In Settings, on the Payments tab, the Card payment link field now links to the guide and to Stripe's own instructions.
- The guide says plainly what one link can and cannot do, so you know the limits before you rely on it.
