- Each of these pages starts with a row of figures: for example how many packages you sell, your lowest price per lesson, or how many people are on your waitlist.
- Prices use your own currency instead of a fixed dollar sign.
- Each coupon shows whether it is active, expired, used up or off.
- A feature that needs a paid plan now shows a small "Pro" or "Academy" tag and a sheet that explains what you get, instead of a blurred page.
- Packages, coupons and plans are checked more carefully when you save them, for example a coupon code must be 3 to 24 letters and numbers.
