- The top of your page shows your photo, rating, languages, time zone and four quick facts: price, lesson lengths, free trial and cancellation.
- A "Book a lesson" card stays beside your introduction. A student picks a length, then a time, then enters their details.
- Your soonest free times are shown as buttons, so a student can book in two taps without opening the calendar.
- Packages, reviews and your booking and cancellation rules sit below your introduction, each in its own clear section.
- The page works the same way on a phone, with the booking card right under your facts.
