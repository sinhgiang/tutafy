- An address that does not exist shows "That page is not here" with four ways back, and its own title, instead of the home page's title.
- The main buttons and the blue bands on the home page, the blog, About, Customers and Updates are a little deeper, so white text on them is easier to read.
- The small labels above headings, the prices on the home page and the contact links reach the 4.5 to 1 contrast rule.
- The Blog, Find tutors, About, Contact, Customer stories and Updates pages now tell search engines and AI assistants what kind of page they are and where they sit on the site.
