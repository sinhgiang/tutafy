title: Say which students you like to teach
summary: In Settings you can tick the kinds of student you like to teach, such as work, exams or travel, so students can find you by what they are learning for.
image: /updates/learning-for.webp

---
- New in Settings, Profile: "Students you like to teach". Tick any of work and career, exams, kids and teens, travel, university and conversation. Leave them all unticked if you would rather not say.
- Students can use it as a filter in the tutor directory ("Learning for"), next to a new price range where they type the lowest and highest price they want.
- When three or more tutors who can be booked teach a language for the same reason, that pair can get its own page, such as English for working professionals. It disappears when there are too few tutors.
- Your ticks are public, like the levels you teach. Nothing else about you is added to your card.
