- On a wide screen, Settings shows a live picture of your booking page beside the form. It changes as you type your name, price, lesson lengths or cancellation rules.
- One bar at the bottom tells you when you have unsaved changes, with Save changes and Discard. If you close the tab by mistake, Tutafy asks first.
- Availability shows your week as a grid, with a Copy a day tool that copies one day's hours onto other days.
- A new free hour that overlaps another on the same day is now refused, with a clear sentence about which one.
- Import shows a table of the first 25 students it found, with notes such as "Already your student", before anything is saved.
- Forms for lessons and students now share one tidy, centred column.
