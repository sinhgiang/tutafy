- A new page, Find me a tutor, asks for the language you want, your level, your price range, when you can have lessons and whether you want a trial lesson.
- It shows the three tutors who fit best, each with the reasons it picked them, their next free time in your time zone, their price and a Book button.
- Every tutor shown has open times in the next 14 days, so each one can be booked today. You do not need an account.
- If fewer than three tutors fit, the page says so and shows what to change to see more. If none fit, you can start again or see everyone.
- The page address remembers your answers, so you can share them. It works in a dark theme that follows your device setting, and on a phone the button stays in reach.
