summary: The Copy link button on a subscription plan now gives a link to your booking page that scrolls to the plans and marks that plan.

---
- On Subscriptions, the copy button on a plan gives you a link to your own booking page for that plan.
- A student who opens it lands on the subscription plans, with your plan marked, and can press Subscribe.
- The button only shows for plans that are switched on, because a student cannot subscribe to a plan that is off.
- If a student adds a plan that is not one of yours to the link, nothing is marked.
