- The menu under your photo holds View as student (when it applies to you), Send feedback, Settings, your Light or Dark choice and Sign out.
- The loose Feedback and theme buttons are gone from the top bar, so it is calmer.
- Refer and earn shows a row of figures, your link with Share and Copy buttons, and how the reward works.
- A table lists the tutors who joined with your link: first name, the day they joined and whether they have upgraded.
- The API and Upgrade pages use the same page header as the rest of the dashboard.
