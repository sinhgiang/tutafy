- If you are a tutor and also take lessons from another tutor, a "View as student" button now appears at the top of your dashboard.
- On the student side, a "Back to tutor" button takes you straight back to your tutor dashboard.
- The button only shows when your sign-in email has been confirmed as yours, so nobody can use it to look at someone else's lessons.
- It also works from the phone menu.
