- New at the top of your dashboard: "Get ready for the tutor directory". It lists three things: choose your free hours, write an introduction of at least 80 characters, and say which languages you teach.
- Each step has a button that opens the right place, and the Settings buttons take you straight to the introduction box or the languages box.
- A bar shows how many of the three steps are done. When all three are done, the card goes away.
- It is only a reminder on your own dashboard. Nothing is sent to you or to anyone else.
