- A graduation cap with an amber tassel in an indigo rounded square, next to the word Tutafy. It replaces the three different marks the product used before.
- It is in the top bar and footer of every page, on sign-in and sign-up, in your tutor app (a lighter version in the dark theme) and in your students' portal.
- It is also on your invoices and certificates, on the "Made with Tutafy" line of your booking page, and in the picture that shows when someone shares a Tutafy link.
- The browser tab icon and the app icon use it too. An app already on your phone's home screen may keep the old icon until you add Tutafy again.
