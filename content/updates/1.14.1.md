title: Easier to read in light and dark
summary: Pale grey text, tiny labels and low-contrast buttons across the tutor app are now clear, and the icon-only buttons have names for screen readers.
image: /updates/legible-text.webp
imageAlt: The Upgrade page with the Pro plan card in a deeper blue, where every line of text is now easy to read
---
- Pale grey text on the Dashboard, student and lesson pages, the forms, AI Tools and Upgrade is darker, so it passes the 4.5 to 1 contrast rule.
- Nothing is smaller than 12px any more: the small day labels, status chips and notes were 9 to 11px.
- On the Upgrade page the Pro plan card is a deeper blue, so its small text and the "Current plan" badge are readable.
- Status chips (scheduled, overdue, active) and green or blue buttons keep their colours but are darker, so the white text on them is clearer.
- The notification count in the top bar is readable in the dark theme.
- The back arrows, the send button in Messages and the add buttons in the lesson form have names for screen readers.
- The weekly chart on the Dashboard no longer puts its "1 lesson" label over the heading.
