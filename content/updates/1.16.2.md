title: The Calendar opens where your lessons are
summary: The week grid scrolls to your first lesson of the week instead of 6 AM, and every field in the lesson, student, team and Settings forms now has a name for screen readers.
image: /updates/calendar-opens-at-lessons.webp
imageAlt: The week view of the Calendar opened at midday, with the day names kept above the hours and two lessons in view
---
- The week view opens one hour before your first lesson of the week, or one hour before now when the week has no lessons. It used to start at 6 AM, so the first screen was mostly empty hours.
- The hours scroll inside the grid and the day names stay above them.
- Hints inside empty fields, like the Zoom link in a lesson, are darker and easy to read.
- Every field in the lesson, student, Team, Messages and Settings forms has a name that a screen reader says, not only a hint inside the box.
- The Google Calendar button in Settings and the Plan card have clearer text.
